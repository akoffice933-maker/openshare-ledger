How it works

Four properties, each checkable

No trust in us required: every claim below is something you can run yourself.

01

Append-only

Each record carries the hash of the previous one. Editing an old entry breaks every record after it — the chain check fails loudly.

02

Recomputable

Points are derived from git history, so anyone can rerun the calculation and compare. Dispute decisions live in a separate chain and never rewrite a contribution.

03

Independently timestamped

Each cycle is sealed with an RFC 3161 timestamp from DigiCert. Verification needs nothing from us — the certificate chain is in your operating system.

04

Attributable

Commit signatures are read from git, not from the commit message, so a forged author field cannot buy credit. Unsigned work is labelled as such.

Legal

What the points are

Not money

Points have no monetary value, cannot be transferred, and give no right to any payment.

Not an offer

Nothing here is an offer, a promise of equity, or an invitation to invest.

Revocable

The project may change the rules, recalculate, or close the programme. The published history stays verifiable either way.

Public record

Contributions are public. Do not send anything you are not allowed to publish.
